# Decisions

Every major choice, when it was made, and why. Newest at the bottom. Reversals are
appended, not edited.

| # | Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives considered |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-10-01 | Budget is $0. No paid APIs, data feeds, hosting tiers or model APIs. | School project; the professor will not accept paid services. | Paid LLM classification (~$150–650 backfill), paid lithium price feeds, Vercel Pro. All excluded. |
| 2 | 2026-10-01 | Pipeline and site are separated: Python pipelines produce versioned Parquet and JSON; the Next.js site reads static JSON. No live database. | Reproducibility, zero hosting cost, fast site. A managed Postgres free tier (0.5 GB) is too small for the news metadata anyway. | Managed Postgres (Neon/Supabase) queried live by the site. |
| 3 | 2026-10-01 | DuckDB + Parquet is the analytical warehouse. Schema documented in docs/PHASE0_PLAN.md section 4 and mirrored in `pipeline/schema`. | Versionable files, zero cost, same SQL schema portable to Postgres later. | Postgres from day one (see 2). |
| 4 | 2026-10-01 | All ingestion and model inference run in GitHub Actions (public repo, unlimited minutes). The development sandbox has no general outbound network access. | The sandbox's egress policy only reaches GitHub and PyPI; Actions runners have open egress and are the scheduled runtime anyway. | Widening the sandbox network policy. |
| 5 | 2026-10-01 | Text classification uses open-weight models only: a multilingual encoder (XLM-R or mDeBERTa) fine-tuned on a hand-coded codebook sample, bootstrapped with zero-shot NLI, trained on Colab/Kaggle free GPUs, run on Actions CPU. The validation sample is coded by the project owner and by Claude inside development sessions (no API calls). | Decision 1. Established method in political text analysis; quality then depends on the coded sample rather than on money. | Anthropic Batch API (Haiku/Sonnet/Opus). |
| 6 | 2026-10-01 | Phase 6 analysis text is template-driven from named indicators plus a separate human-written interpretation layer. | Every sentence traces to an indicator; more defensible academically than generated prose; free. | Open-model-drafted prose on Colab (still possible later, optional). |
| 7 | 2026-10-01 | Hosting on the project owner's existing Vercel account (Hobby tier, free `*.vercel.app` URL), site root directory `web/`. | Decision 1; non-commercial use qualifies for Hobby. | Cloudflare Pages (OpenNext adapter needed), Vercel Pro. |
| 8 | 2026-10-01 | Map boundaries from Natural Earth 1:10m `admin_0_map_units` (public domain) so French Guiana is its own polygon, rendered and labeled as French territory outside the analysis. | `admin_0_countries` merges French Guiana into France; `topojson/world-atlas` is archived (Natural Earth 4.1). | world-atlas npm package. |
| 9 | 2026-10-01 | Mineral list and HS6 mapping live in `pipeline/config/minerals.yaml` and `hs_codes.yaml`; default UI set is the nine minerals from the brief, extended set covers other US/Chinese list minerals produced or explored in the region. The 2025 USGS list (60 minerals) is the reference, so copper and silver count as critical. | Brief section 3 asks for a configurable list. | Hard-coding in the UI. |
| 10 | 2026-10-01 | Source registry is `pipeline/config/sources.yaml`; `SOURCES.md` and the site's Sources page are generated from it and never edited by hand. | Single source of truth for URL, coverage, license, reliability rating, refresh schedule. | Maintaining SOURCES.md manually. |
| 11 | 2026-10-01 | Dead or moved sources from the brief are replaced as listed in docs/PHASE0_PLAN.md section 2.1 (IMF DOTS→IMTS; BU/Dialogue Chinese loans→BU CODF + AidData; EXIM→data.gov CSV; MSP→FORGE; Georgetown PDBA→SIGLA; UNCTAD bilateral FDI→IMF DIP). | Verified during Phase 0 (search-based; direct checks happen in the first Actions run). | Keeping dead sources listed as gaps. |
| 12 | 2026-10-01 | SEDAR+ and HKEXnews are never scraped; their terms prohibit it. Canadian and Hong Kong filings come from SEC cross-listings and company investor-relations pages. | Brief section 4: respect terms of use. | Community scrapers that violate the terms. |
| 13 | 2026-10-01 | Lithium prices: USGS annual averages plus customs-derived unit values (value ÷ quantity for HS 2836.91 and 2825.20), labeled "implicit realized price". | No free, licensed lithium benchmark exists; paid feeds excluded by decision 1. | Scraping investing.com or Trading Economics (terms prohibit). |
| 14 | 2026-10-01 | Phase 1 sample data is generated by a seeded script (`pipeline/scripts/make_sample_data.py`), stored under `web/public/data/sample/`, and every panel shows a SAMPLE DATA banner until real data replaces it. | Brief section 6; prevents sample numbers being mistaken for findings. | Hand-written fixtures. |
