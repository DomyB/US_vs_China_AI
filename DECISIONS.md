# Decisions

Every major choice, when it was made, and why. Newest at the bottom. Reversals are
appended, not edited.

| # | Date | Decision | Why | Alternatives considered |

| 1 | 2026-10-01 | Budget is $0. No paid APIs, data feeds, hosting tiers or model APIs. | School project; the professor will not accept paid services. | Paid LLM classification (~$150–650 backfill), paid lithium price feeds, Vercel Pro. All excluded. |
| 2 | 2026-10-01 | Pipeline and site are separated: Python pipelines produce versioned Parquet and JSON; the Next.js site reads static JSON. | 3 | 2026-10-01 | DuckDB + Parquet is the analytical warehouse. Schema documented in docs/PHASE0_PLAN.md section 4 and mirrored in `pipeline/schema`. | Versionable files, zero cost, same SQL schema portable to Postgres later. | Postgres from day one (see 2). |
| 4 | 2026-10-01 | All ingestion and model inference run in GitHub Actions (public repo, unlimited minutes). | 5 | 2026-10-01 | Text classification uses open-weight models only: a multilingual encoder (XLM-R or mDeBERTa) fine-tuned on a hand-coded codebook sample, bootstrapped with zero-shot NLI, trained on Colab/Kaggle free GPUs, run on Actions CPU. | 43 | 2026-10-04 | Stance applicability (which records are scored toward which actor) is decided at classification time with the current keyword rule applied to the text, not with the flags stored at ingestion; the US rule now blanks currency phrases ("dólares dos Estados Unidos da América", US$, USD), Mexico's official name and firm or region names containing "America" before matching. | 45 | 2026-10-09 | The influence index (Phase 4) is built from six observable ties per actor (export share to the actor, import share from it, documented official-finance commitments over three years / GDP, debt owed to it / GDP, UN General Assembly voting agreement, legislative stance from the text model with ≥5 scored records), normalised by winsorised min–max over the whole panel with both actors on one scale, aggregated with equal weights renormalised over the components that exist, never from fewer than three, and never imputed: a missing input leaves the component unavailable with the reason stored (`index_component.note`). Uncertainty is a 500-draw band (Dirichlet weights around equal, percentile-rank normalisation in half of the draws; 5th–95th percentile) and the rank stability is published. Also computed per mineral where the country trades it (the two trade components become mineral-specific). | OECD/JRC handbook steps, with the choices the data allows: 300 country-years cannot support PCA weights; one pooled scale makes a US value and a Chinese value comparable (US values are low because US ties are small, which is the finding); imputation would invent ties. | PCA or expert weights; per-actor normalisation (hides the asymmetry); imputing missing components with zeros or means. |
| 46 | 2026-10-09 | Central-bank swap-line drawdowns (AidData rows whose description names a swap) are excluded from the finance component and shown as `rescue_lending` flags; only rows AidData recommends for aggregates (`confidence = documented`) and DFC/OPIC rows count. | The PBoC–BCRA drawdowns are US$113 bn of the US$455 bn recorded for the region and are liquidity support, not project finance; counting them would make Argentina's finance tie to China ten times Brazil's. | Counting all rows; a separate emergency-liquidity component. |
| 47 | 2026-10-09 | A Herfindahl index of export destinations is not computed: the warehouse holds partner totals for the United States, China and the world only, so `concentration.hhi_export_dest` is stored as null with that note; shares, the two-power share and a revealed comparative advantage against the pooled twelve-country basket are computed instead. A partner row absent from a reporter's Comtrade answer for a year it did report is read as no recorded flow and noted (`share_* note`). | Honesty rule: nothing estimated silently. All-partner flows become fetchable once the `COMTRADE_KEY` secret exists (Phase 4b). | Approximating the HHI from three buckets (a degenerate measure); treating absent partner rows as missing (drops every zero flow and biases shares upward). |
| 48 | 2026-10-09 | Anomaly flags are deterministic rules on the sourced series with the evidence level fixed by what they rest on: `documented` (a share of exports to an actor moving ≥20 points in a year with ≥US$100 m exported in both years; documented commitments ≥1% of GDP in a year; swap drawdowns), `strongly_indicated` (commitments ≥0.5% of GDP with no change in the actor's trade share within two years; the debt stock owed to China rising ≥20% and ≥US$100 m in a year with no commitment recorded), `speculative` (a say–do gap beyond two standard deviations, which rests on the text model). Every flag names its source ids. | PHASE0_PLAN §4.4 asks for evidence levels; rules are auditable and reproducible, unlike an outlier model on 12 countries. Mirror-discrepancy and third-country-subsidiary flags wait for mirror trade and ownership data. | Statistical outlier detection (isolation forest etc.) on so few units. |
| 49 | 2026-10-09 | The finance network is the lender–recipient graph of the finance events (funding institutions linked to the receiving agencies named in each record, weighted by amount; centralities on the twelve-country graph, eigenvector centrality on the largest connected component, greedy-modularity communities), stored in `network_metric` and `network_edge`; events naming no recipient are left out and counted. The say–do gap uses the legislative stance only (≥5 scored records per year) against the year-on-year change of the economic-ties sub-index, and carries the text model's status label. | The contracts table has no company names and cadastres exist for Ecuador only, so ownership links cannot be built yet; headlines are too few for a media rhetoric series. | Attaching unattributed events to a per-country "unspecified" hub (would dominate every country's centrality); mixing media stance in with 28 headlines. |
