# US–China Competition for Critical Minerals in South America (2008–2026)

A public research platform that tracks, analyses and forecasts the competition between
the United States and China for critical minerals in the twelve sovereign states of
South America. For each country and year it combines three layers:

1. **Actions**: what governments and companies actually do (trade, investment, loans,
   concessions, contracts, offtake agreements, infrastructure, diplomatic agreements).
2. **Parliament**: what is said, proposed and voted in each national legislature.
3. **Media**: how each country's own press covers it.

On top of these, the site produces an influence index, flags under-reported or
indirect influence, and makes forecasts to 2030 with stated uncertainty.

**Facts (trade, finance, contracts, governance, prices, policy documents) are real** where the
status banner says so. Parliament and media records are real; their stance, tone, translations and
narratives are model outputs labelled with the classifier's validation status (zero-shot baseline
until the hand-coded sample exists); indices and forecasts are still generated by a seeded script
and labelled SAMPLE DATA until Phases 4–5.

## Ground rules

- Every number and claim links back to its source. Sources carry a reliability rating:
  official, independent/academic, partisan, or state-controlled media.
- Missing data is shown as missing. Estimated data is labelled with its method.
- The interface separates facts (sourced data), model outputs (indices, forecasts) and
  interpretation (written analysis).
- Original-language text is kept alongside any translation, with a link to the original.
- For news, only headline, date, outlet, URL and derived analysis are stored.
- Scraping respects robots.txt, terms of use and rate limits; official APIs and bulk
  downloads are preferred. Sources whose terms forbid automated access (SEDAR+, HKEX)
  are not scraped.
- The whole project runs at zero cost: free tiers, open data and open-weight models only.

## Running locally

```bash
# site
cd web && pnpm install && pnpm dev
# regenerate SOURCES.md and the site's sources.json from the registry
python3 pipeline/scripts/build_sources.py
```
