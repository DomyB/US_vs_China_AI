## Risks and likely next moves

The published models put China's share at 27% in 2030 at baseline, 37% under an accelerated China pull and 17% if US sourcing rules bite; the US share reaches 11% only under reshoring [forecast:export_share:CN:baseline:2030:ARG] [forecast:export_share:US:us_reshoring:2030:ARG]. Parity is cheap in Argentina because the US buys so little: US$319 m of yearly exports switching from China would equalise the two, 54 times what the United States bought in 2025, and lithium alone could cover it [insights:parity_gap:ARG]. The likely next move, in my reading, is that the November 2025 trade framework and the RIGI regime bring US-origin finance to copper and lithium projects, which would show first in the DFC record and in the statements, long before it shows in trade [event:us_arg_swap_line_2025] [event:arg_ley_bases_rigi].
