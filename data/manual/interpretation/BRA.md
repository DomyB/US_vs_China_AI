My reading: Brazil talks about the minerals of the next decade in the language of sovereignty, and sells the minerals of this one to China.

## Risks and likely next moves

The models put China's share at 52% in 2030 at baseline, 62% under the China pull and 42% under US reshoring; the US share reaches 12% only under reshoring [forecast:export_share:CN:us_reshoring:2030:BRA] [forecast:export_share:US:us_reshoring:2030:BRA]. Parity would take US$10.7 bn of yearly exports switching, 8.8 times what the United States bought in 2025; iron ore alone could cover it, nothing else could [insights:parity_gap:BRA]. The law of 16 September 2026 creating the national critical-minerals policy and its council is the next move already made [event:bra_lei_15506_critical_minerals]; in my reading it will shape where rare earths, niobium and graphite go in the 2030s, not where iron ore goes in the 2020s. The risk for the reading is a steel-demand shift in China that moves the iron-ore share for reasons that have nothing to do with policy.
