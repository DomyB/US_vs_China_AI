## Alignment

Suriname has the deepest China lean in the region, 45.6 against 7.6 in 2024, on four components each [index_value:influence:CN:2024] [index_value:influence:US:2024]. The lean does not come from trade: the US$1.6 bn of mineral exports in 2025 are gold with no reported flow to either power [concentration:share_cn_x:2025] [flow_trade:SUR:gold:2025]. It comes from money and votes: US$747 m of Chinese commitments over 2015–2021, nine of them flagged as large relative to GDP, seven as rescue lending and seven as finance without a trade response, and no US commitment [flow_finance:CN:SUR:2015-2021] [anomaly_flag:large_commitment] [anomaly_flag:rescue_lending] [flow_finance:US:SUR:2015-2021]. No statement, legislative record or headline is coded [coverage:SUR]. My reading: Suriname's dependence is a debt relationship that the mineral trade does not show, and the index records it correctly as influence of a different kind.

## Trajectory
